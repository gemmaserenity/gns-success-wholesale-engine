START HERE

The complete deal-research workflow

Advance only when the prior handoff is traceable.

  1. 01

    Discover a distress signal

    Find a Notice of Trustee's Sale, delinquent-tax signal, or another approved public-record event. Save its exact URL and record number.

  2. 02

    Resolve the parcel

    Match county, APN, situs address, owner name, and legal/property identity across assessor and recorder records.

  3. 03

    Verify event and ownership

    Read the actual recorded notice and current deed. Confirm dates, parties, document numbers, and any ownership mismatch.

  4. 04

    Research debt, taxes, and liens

    Use treasurer and recorder evidence for preliminary ranges. Obtain payoff and title evidence before relying on exact closing numbers.

  5. 05

    Research property and value

    Collect physical facts, sold comparable evidence, occupancy/HOA evidence, and a supportable repair range.

  6. 06

    Run preliminary economics

    Enter ranges in Manual evaluation. Let the deterministic engine calculate scenarios, deal killers, score, and next action.

  7. 07

    Add property evidence

    For a saved opportunity, record each fact with provider, source URL, classification, confidence, retrieval time, and cost.

  8. 08

    Calculate buyer demand

    Compare the opportunity with active, contact-eligible buyer profiles. Missing constrained evidence remains unknown, not matched.

  9. 09

    Selective contact research

    Only for a specifically qualified opportunity: document necessity, identity basis, privacy review, source, cost ceiling, and existing suppression before opening one research case.

  10. 10

    Human qualification and diligence

    Review each finding, record contact standing separately, verify seller authority, then complete the acquisition checklist. Contact data alone never authorizes outreach, and checklist readiness is not offer authorization.

  11. 11

    Internal term authorization

    A verified Access operator may authorize bounded internal ceilings against the current ready review. Authority expires or becomes stale; it creates no document and permits no outreach.

SOURCE DIRECTORY

Where to look first

Open the exact parcel or document page and save that URL whenever possible.

Maricopa County

Assessor property searchAPN, situs address, current owner, mailing address, property characteristics, assessed value, deed reference. Treasurer property-tax informationTax bills, amounts due, payment status, delinquent years, and tax-lien indicators. Recorder document searchDeeds, deeds of trust, releases, liens, judgments, affidavits of value, and Notices of Trustee's Sale. Assessor parcel mapParcel location, boundaries, lot context, and map references. Verify zoning with the governing city or county.

Pinal County

Assessor parcel searchAPN, property address, owner, mailing address, parcel description, and property characteristics. Treasurer parcel inquiryTax amounts due, bills, payment history, receipts, and tax-sale information. The site states that data updates nightly. Recorder official-records searchSearch recorded documents by party, fee number, document type, date, legal description, or docket/page. Parcel zoning reportParcel size, owner/location context, current zoning, and special-use records. Confirm with the governing planning office.

Arizona and professional verification

Arizona business-entity searchEntity status, principals, statutory agent, and filed entity documents when title is held by an LLC or corporation. Arizona court case lookupPotential civil-case signals. The site is not the official court record and excludes some case types, including probate. U.S. Bankruptcy Court — District of ArizonaBankruptcy case resources and PACER access. Bankruptcy is a mandatory human/legal review flag.
Title/escrow, lender payoff, contractor, MLS or permitted comparable providerUse these for full title evidence, current payoff, repair scope, and market-supported ARV. They are not replaced by county websites.

ENGINE OUTPUTS

What the app calculates

These are outputs; researchers do not type them into source fields.

Base inputs

Base ARV = (ARV low + ARV high) ÷ 2

Base repairs = (repairs low + repairs high) ÷ 2

Base debt = (debt low + debt high) ÷ 2

Investor economics

Investor ceiling = ARV × 0.78 − repairs − $12,000 risk buffer

Estimated contract = proposed contract; otherwise debt + liens + $5,000 seller-net floor

Wholesale outputs

Maximum contract = investor ceiling − $10,000 target assignment

Expected assignment = investor ceiling − estimated contract

Estimated equity = ARV − debt − liens

Buyer matching

Target buyer price = base estimated contract + $10,000 target assignment

Buyer demand is recalculated from probable-buyer breadth plus fit and credibility. Missing constrained property facts produce possible—not probable—matches.

The 0.78 factor, $12,000 risk buffer, $5,000 seller-net floor, and $10,000 assignment target are current named configuration defaults—not universal market laws. Downside uses low ARV/high repairs/high debt; upside uses high ARV/low repairs/low debt.

AI SQUAD

Agent ownership and handoffs

Each agent produces evidence; no agent silently fills another agent's gap.

A1

Discovery Agent

Finds: approved distress records and candidate parcels.

Hands off: source, source URL, record ID, retrieval time, raw notice/document.

Stops when: source is unauthorized, inaccessible, duplicate, or cannot identify a parcel.

A2

Parcel & Identity Agent

Finds: county, APN, situs address, current owner, mailing address, current deed reference.

Hands off: normalized parcel identity plus owner-confidence and mismatch flag.

Never decides: that the apparent contact has authority to sell.

A3

Distress, Debt & Title Agent

Finds: trustee notice, scheduled date, recorded loans/releases, taxes, liens, entity/court warning signals.

Hands off: debt/liens ranges, source documents, title-complexity flags.

Never declares: exact payoff or clear title without professional evidence.

A4

Property Evidence Agent

Finds: type, size, beds/baths, year, lot, assessment, transfer, HOA/occupancy evidence.

Hands off: one evidence envelope per source with classification and confidence.

Never infers: occupancy from mailing address alone.

A5

Valuation & Condition Agent

Finds: relevant sold comps, ARV range, visible condition, repair scope/range.

Hands off: comp set, adjustments, photos/inspection/contractor support, uncertainty.

Never substitutes: assessed value for ARV.

A6

Underwriting Agent

Does: validates required ranges and runs deterministic scenarios and deal-killer rules.

Hands off: immutable evaluation, score, reasons, confidence, and next action.

Never changes: formulas ad hoc or invents a missing input.

A7

Buyer Demand Agent

Does: compares a non-rejected opportunity with active buyer buy boxes and property evidence.

Hands off: probable/possible/excluded/ineligible classifications and criterion explanations.

Never contacts: a buyer without eligible contact standing and operator authorization.

A8

Selective Contact Research Agent

Does: opens one gated research case only after purpose, necessity, owner identity, privacy, suppression, and cost checks pass.

Hands off: immutable findings with source, retrieval time, confidence, cost, and identity status.

Never: bulk-enriches, calls a provider from this app, or treats a contact value as consent.

A9

QA & Compliance Agent

Checks: provenance, recency, conflicts, confidence, duplicate counting, consent, and do-not-contact status.

Hands off: approved research packet or an explicit correction queue.

Escalates: probate, bankruptcy, mismatch, legal/title ambiguity, and unsupported high-confidence claims.

A10

Seller Intake & Acquisitions

Does later: permitted seller intake, authority/motivation discovery, appointment, offer discussion, disclosures, and human negotiation.

Hands off: seller-verified facts and acquisition decision.

Requires: eligible contact standing, compliance gates, and human oversight before outreach or contracting.

Required handoff packet

Every agent handoff must include: county + APN, field name, original value, normalized value, provider/source, exact source URL, source record ID when available, retrieval timestamp, evidence classification, confidence, cost, conflicts, and the next unresolved question. Business state belongs in the database—not only in an agent conversation.

QUALITY CONTROL

Evidence classifications and completion gates

Confidence describes support—not optimism.

Public record

A fact copied from an identifiable government record. Preserve the document/parcel link and retrieval time.

Verified

Supported by a reliable source suitable for the fact, such as payoff, title, closing, or permitted provider evidence.

Human verified

A qualified person checked the source or condition and recorded what was reviewed.

Estimated

A bounded working value with method and uncertainty. Use ranges; lower data confidence appropriately.

Model derived

Calculated from named inputs and a documented model version. Preserve inputs and never present it as observed fact.

Ready for preliminary evaluation

  • County, APN, address, and owner resolve.
  • Distress source is traceable.
  • ARV, repairs, and debt ranges are present.
  • Known liens and confidence are addressed.
  • Conflicts are flagged, not hidden.

Ready for buyer-demand matching

  • Opportunity is persisted and not rejected.
  • Relevant constrained property facts are recorded.
  • Buyer profiles are active and evidence-based.
  • Contact standing and credibility are current.
  • Unknown facts remain explicitly unknown.

Ready for selective contact research

  • Opportunity is qualified, scores at least 80, and supports the target spread.
  • Owner identity confidence is at least 65%.
  • Purpose, necessity, privacy, source, and cost are documented.
  • Public/property evidence and contact standing were reviewed.
  • No active do-not-contact suppression exists.

Not ready for contract or closing

  • Seller identity or authority is uncertain.
  • Sale date/postponement is not current.
  • Payoff, taxes, liens, or title remain materially uncertain.
  • Probate/bankruptcy/legal issues lack review.
  • Required disclosures or human approval are missing.

This page is an operational research guide, not legal, tax, appraisal, lending, title, or contracting advice. County and court systems can be incomplete, delayed, or changed. Verify material facts with the responsible agency or qualified professional.
